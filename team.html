Our People

A Team Built on Accountability & Excellence

NSW is powered by experienced professionals who bring technical expertise, personal accountability, and genuine care to every project. From the office to the field, our team operates as a cohesive unit — guided by our core values and a relentless commitment to delivering for our clients.

Clint Morgan

Founder & President

His leadership philosophy centers on empowering people, fostering accountability, and creating a family oriented culture where individuals are encouraged to grow and succeed. Clint remains actively involved in shaping the company's vision, strengthening strategic partnerships, and ensuring NSWIS stays true to the values on which it was founded.

General Manager

Jerry believes that long term success is built on trust, integrity, and consistently delivering on commitments.

Our Culture

What It Means to Be Part of NSW

Team First

Every member of NSW contributes to the team's success. We look out for each other, support each other's growth, and celebrate wins together.

Continuous Learning

Technology evolves constantly. NSW invests in ongoing training, manufacturer certifications, and apprenticeship programs to keep our team at the leading edge.

Join Our Team

NSW Integrated Systems is always looking for talented, motivated individuals who share our values and commitment to excellence. As an IBEW Local 569 signatory contractor, we offer union wages, benefits, and access to the IBEW apprenticeship program — one of the most respected trade apprenticeship tracks in the industry.

Whether you're an experienced low-voltage technician, a project manager, or entering the trade through the IBEW apprenticeship, we want to hear from you.
