Our Story

Southern California's Integrated Systems Leader

NSW Integrated Systems was founded with a simple but powerful idea: that clients deserve an integrated systems contractor they can trust unconditionally — one that delivers complex, mission-critical systems with precision, accountability, and care.

Based in San Diego, CA, NSW has grown into one of Southern California's most respected low-voltage and integrated security contractors. We serve commercial campuses, healthcare networks, transit authorities, utilities, and government facilities across the region.

Our team includes IBEW Local 569 signatory field personnel — trained tradespeople who bring union-quality craftsmanship to every installation. Combined with our manufacturer certifications and dual licensure (C-10 Electrical + ACO Alarm Company), we offer clients a uniquely capable and accountable partner.

We believe that a great company is built on great people, and great people thrive in a culture that values family, accountability, and integrity. Those aren't buzzwords at NSW — they're the foundation of everything we do.

"A company is only as good as the team that comprises the company. Through a focus on creating a culture of not only excellence but personal accountability, the value and importance of family, and the empowerment of our team members, we have succeeded in building a team that is second to none."
— Clint Morgan, President, NSW Integrated Systems

Family

We treat our team like family and our clients the same. The relationships we build are long-term, personal, and grounded in genuine care.

Accountability

We own our work, our mistakes, and our outcomes. Personal accountability is a non-negotiable expectation at every level of our organization.

Integrity

We do what we say we'll do, every time. Integrity means being honest in our communications, transparent in our pricing, and consistent in our quality.

Trust

Our clients trust us with critical infrastructure. We earn and protect that trust through professionalism, reliability, and discretion.

Honesty
